This is demonstration content, not a new account of Ben's professional experience. It illustrates how a future article can begin with a question and develop an explanation.

## Start with a clear question

A useful discussion can begin by naming the decision in front of the team. What outcome matters? What is already known, and what still needs investigation?

> **Discussion prompt:** Describe the decision before proposing another process. This is an illustrative prompt, not a claim about a delivered result.

## Make the next step understandable

An example next step might identify an owner, a question to investigate, and a point at which to compare what was learned. The example does not claim a metric or a particular historical outcome.

### Leave room to learn

Markdown keeps the explanation editable. Headings automatically populate the contents navigation; no additional page component is needed.
