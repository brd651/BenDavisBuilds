This sample demonstrates the archive format. It is not an imported historical article, and its text does not document a real product, command, or professional achievement.

## Preserve the original context

When genuine historical material is added, its original publication date belongs in the metadata. Later context can explain what changed without silently presenting old guidance as current.

## Read with care

The archive notice provides a visible reminder that technical guidance can become obsolete. For this sample there is no historical date to verify, and none has been invented.
