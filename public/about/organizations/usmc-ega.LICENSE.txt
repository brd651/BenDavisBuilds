# United States Marine Corps emblem

`usmc-ega.svg` is the unmodified Eagle, Globe and Anchor vector from
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eagle,_Globe_and_Anchor.svg
(original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3a/Eagle%2C_Globe_and_Anchor.svg).

The source identifies it as a U.S. Marine Corps work in the U.S. public domain.
It appears solely beside the author's documented Marine Corps service on the
experimental career timeline. It does not imply Marine Corps endorsement.
The official government seal is not used. The Built Signal identity is unchanged.
